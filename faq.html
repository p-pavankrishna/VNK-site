Frequently Asked Questions.

Clear, transparent information about one-on-one personal training at your community gym, coaching structure, and what to expect.

One-on-one personal training begins with an initial consultation to understand your goals, training history, and lifestyle. VNK then conducts a movement assessment to design a structured strength and mobility routine specifically for you. During sessions, VNK coaches you one-on-one at your community gym, focusing on technique, pacing, safety, and appropriate progression.
Training sessions take place directly at clients' community or residential gyms. This provides the comfort and convenience of training in your own environment without having to commute to a commercial gym.
VNK Personal Training primarily serves working professionals seeking focused, efficient strength training around busy work schedules, and senior citizens aiming to build strength, mobility, and confidence for active daily living.
